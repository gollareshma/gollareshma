### 📌 Featured projects

#### 🎥 [AI Video Intelligence](https://github.com/gollareshma/AI_Video_Intelligence)

**Search hours of CCTV footage in plain language — typed or spoken.**

Most surveillance footage is empty, so the system prunes cheaply before doing anything expensive. Detection
and tracking cut long video into entity-centric clips, captioning and CLIP embeddings make them searchable,
and a LangGraph agent decomposes the query, searches across cameras, and applies temporal filters to return
the matching clip cut at the right timestamp.

**15.1% t-IoU** and **70.4% Top-K** on ForeSeaQA against 13.6 / 66.0 for the published baseline — on a
single T4, with no video-LLM inference anywhere in the pipeline.

`Python` `PyTorch` `YOLOv8` `ByteTrack` `CLIP` `LangGraph` `FastAPI` `ChromaDB` `PostgreSQL` `Docker`

#### ☸️ [Kubernetes Voting App](https://github.com/gollareshma/K8s-voting-app)

**A microservices app, plus the operational layer that makes it survivable.**

Five services on Kubernetes with CI/CD, horizontal autoscaling, zero-trust network policies, centralised
logging, and Prometheus/Grafana dashboards. Then five chaos experiments — pod kills, network partitions,
resource starvation — each with a written post-mortem, scoring 8.5/10 on resilience.

`Kubernetes` `GitHub Actions` `Docker` `Prometheus` `Grafana`

#### 🐧 [LAMP Stack DevOps](https://github.com/gollareshma/lamp-stack-devops-project)

**The same server, provisioned two ways.**

A full LAMP stack built first with a Bash script, then with an idempotent Ansible playbook — a direct
comparison of scripting a server versus declaring one. Run the script twice and it breaks; run the playbook
twice and nothing changes. That difference is the whole point.

`Ansible` `Bash` `AWS EC2` `Apache` `MySQL`

#### 🛒 [Instacart Market Basket Analysis](https://github.com/gollareshma/Instacart-Market-Basket-Analysis)

**3.4 million grocery orders, 206,000 customers, one useful finding.**

Reorder rates jump **27 percentage points** between a customer's first ten orders and their eleventh
through twentieth, then flatten. Attrition peaks just before that window. Together those say something
specific: retention effort spent on the first ten orders is worth more than the same effort spent later.

`Python` `pandas` `NumPy` `Matplotlib` `Seaborn`

#### 📉 [Startup Profitability Prediction](https://github.com/gollareshma/Startup-Profitability-Prediction)

**Can funding and market share predict profitability? On this data, no — and that's the finding.**

Logistic Regression and Random Forest compared on 500 startups. Both land near 0.53 test AUC, essentially
random. Tuning fixed the forest's overfitting without producing predictive power. Two very different model
classes converging on the same non-answer points at the data, not the models — there are no cost or
time-series features here, and profitability is fundamentally about costs over time.

`Python` `scikit-learn` `pandas` `Seaborn`
